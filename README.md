# Pharmaceutical-Supply-Chain-Inventory-Inventory-Optimization

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to apply data science and machine learning techniques to optimise inventory management within the pharmaceutical supply chain. The project focuses on analysing historical product sales, market trends, shelf-life constraints and safety stock requirements to support better inventory planning and demand forecasting.

By developing predictive models and applying inventory optimisation techniques, the project aims to identify appropriate stock levels for different products, reduce the risk of overstocking and minimise potential losses caused by expired or unsold products.

Ultimately, the project seeks to provide data-driven insights and practical recommendations that can help pharmaceutical companies improve inventory control, reduce waste, manage stock more efficiently and enhance overall supply chain performance.
